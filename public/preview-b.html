马 尾 · Mǎ Wěi · Horsetail

The Shui people developed their own pictographic script — one of the few writing systems in the world created and maintained exclusively by women. These two characters, 马尾, name the very material at the heart of the craft.

马
Horse
尾
Tail
Drawn in the pictographic style of Shuishu, the ancient script of the Shui people

Every
motif
speaks

Shui embroidery is a symbolic language. Fish bring abundance. Butterflies carry transformation. The vine signals continuity across generations.
